## Direction contract

THESIS: He is the product. A pinned navy stage where his real portrait, rebuilt as a depth mesh, turns, rises and pushes in as you scroll, the way a phone is shown on a launch page. Refuses the soft paper collage the site wears now and the split hero with a round avatar.

OWN-WORLD: Navy stage with a navy-2 spotlight and faint navy-2 grid, bone type, one gold rim light and gold fills for actions. Below the stage, cloud and paper sections with navy Bricolage 800 headlines, 1px and 2px navy rules, 6px corners, no hard offset shadows, no stickers, no tape.

STORY: The visitor meets him, reads the hook, watches him turn while the owner's lines land one by one (same skills, one move, one system, one invoice), then reaches receipts, the builds, the offer and the process, and acts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed navy. His head and shoulders centre, head top near 14% height, chest fading into the ground. A giant tone-on-tone BIG QUIV wordmark sits behind his head. The owner's headline in bone Bricolage 800 across the lower third, gold-marked phrase, Join the waitlist (gold fill) and Hire me beside it.

FORM: Apple product scroll after the Adewura build method (sticky 600svh stage, keyframed pose, ghost-trail headlines, Lenis plus GSAP ScrollTrigger), brief-pinned, no concept-seed roll (direction pinned by owner and brand system).
