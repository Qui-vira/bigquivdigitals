## Platform

web

## Users

Three visitors, in order of value to the business:

1. A founder, brand or project lead (crypto exchange, Web3 project, AI startup, fintech, health brand) deciding whether to hire one execution partner for growth. They need to answer four questions in under five minutes: who is this, what problem does he solve, why trust him, how do I hire him.
2. A learner who wants a skill that pays, or has a skill that is not paying yet (the two course buyers).
3. A creator or editor buying TheEditCompanion, a downloadable Claude Code motion-design pack.

## Product Purpose

bigquivdigitals.com sells one service offer, two courses and one downloadable tool, and carries the proof behind each. Success is a booked scope call, a course purchase or waitlist sign-up, or a TheEditCompanion purchase.

## Positioning

Big Quiv is a Nigerian Web3 and digital growth operator who builds the systems that turn attention into leads, users, community and revenue. One execution partner and one system instead of five freelancers and five invoices. Every claim on the site is backed by a screenshot, a live link or a checkable number.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offers on the site, and only these: the Growth Operating System (quote by scope, sold by call); The Great Work (course, waitlist); the AI class (course, name not locked, so no public name is invented); TheEditCompanion (download, own product page).
- Retired offers never return under any name (positioning doc section 3).
- Existing infrastructure stays: Flutterwave and Blockradar payments, auth, admin, the Telegram course gate, `/doc/[slug]` articles, the waitlist API. Public pages only are redesigned.
- Next.js 16 on Vercel project `website`. Nothing deploys without the owner's approval.

## Product Principles

1. Nothing ships without evidence behind it.
2. One page, one buyer, one promise.
3. The owner's own copy is kept word for word; design changes how it looks, not what it says.
4. A buyer can reach the action in one click from any viewport.
