What This Prompt Creates
------------------------

A 15-second ultra realistic celebrity airport arrival scene. Single continuous handheld shot from the crowd perspective. A celebrity walks through a packed crowd with bodyguards, waves to fans, enters a Lamborghini Aventador with a full convoy, and drives away. Documentary-style realism with natural audio, lighting, and camera shake.

---

What the Full Prompt Covers
---------------------------

* **Audio** direction (crowd, shutters, announcements, engine sounds)
* **Lighting** setup (natural daylight, glass reflections, haze)
* **Character** lock with face, outfit, and accessory details
* **Outfit strict lock** to prevent AI drift across frames
* **6-beat shot sequence** (0-3s, 3-6s, 6-9s, 9-11s, 11-13.5s, 13.5-15s) with exact camera and action directions
* **Action rules** to prevent the AI from skipping key moments

---

Why It Works
------------

This prompt uses documentary-style camera constraints (handheld, crowd POV, no cuts) to force the AI into realism. The outfit strict lock prevents the model from changing clothing between frames. The action rules at the end explicitly tell the model which moments it cannot skip, which is the key to getting a complete scene instead of a half-rendered one.
