The Journey

start here

1. ![The Beginning](data:image/jpeg;base64,<inline 38766 bytes stripped>)

   2018

   2018

   ### The Beginning

   I come from a very poor home. Not the inspirational kind that people romanticize.

   The real kind where you count rice grains and pray that tomorrow will be kinder.

   My parents provided the basics. Food, clothes, shelter.

   But I always knew it was survival, not freedom.

   I watched kids on the Island spend without thinking twice.

   I wanted that life, but nothing around me looked like it was meant for someone like me.

   I did everything society told me to do.

   Get good grades.

   Get a nice 9 to 5.

   Join the rat race.

   That path failed me.

   After graduating as a microbiologist, I ended up as a sales boy in my father’s shop earning ten thousand naira per month.

   Watching my peers move forward while I felt stuck.

   Rejected by big firms because I had no connection.

   Depressed in silence because I had no one to tell.

   At some point, survival pushed me into circles that tried to lure me into fraud and even darker things.

   I ran for my life and hid with relatives because I knew their secrets and they wanted me gone.

   That was the lowest point of my story.
2. ![Web Design Class](data:image/jpeg;base64,<inline 38766 bytes stripped>)

   2019

   2019

   ### Web Design Class

   Then everything changed.

   A friend asked me a simple question that shifted my life forever.

   “Why not learn a skill?”

   I chose web design. I went all in. I got cheated, underpaid, mocked, and still kept going. I sold mobile data to survive while building my knowledge brick by brick. My first real break was a forty thousand naira website job. Then a project for a senator who did not even pay me in full, yet I was grateful because something finally felt possible.
3. ![WEB3 Exposure](data:image/jpeg;base64,<inline 42654 bytes stripped>)

   2020

   2020

   ### WEB3 Exposure

   That small fire led me to bigger rooms.

   I met people who opened my eyes to online income, affiliate marketing, and crypto opportunities. I joined Forsage and made my first million. I tasted what freedom could look like. Then I lost everything through futures trading and got liquidated. That pain dragged me into DeFi. This time, I studied. I learned. I rebuilt myself from scratch.

   From the ashes of confusion, I found clarity.

   From the ruins of losses, I found purpose.

   I mastered on chain analysis, market behavior, data systems, and blockchain development. I no longer wanted to be a spectator. I wanted to build the stage. I trained myself in Solidity. Audited smart contracts. Built dashboards used by thousands. Became the person I once prayed to meet.

   2021

   ### Ophir Institute

   I built Ophir Institute

   A crazy idea at the time.Today it is one of the fastest rising dev institutes in Web3.

   Registered in Nigeria as Ophir Digital Education Foundation, CAC 9071886.

   The foundation is a non-profit. The Great Work, my paid course, is a separate BigQuiv Digitals product.

   Over 2,000 students trained.Graduates landing high value jobs, winning hackathons, and building products across ecosystems like Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, Fantom, Flow, Base, and Scroll.

   Together they have made over two hundred thousand dollars.

   Some of their stories still shock me.

   Ayola Babs lost everything on Binance. He now owns a fully furnished apartment and a car.

   Another joined my zero to five thousand program with three hundred thousand naira left and tripled it.

   Bernard made half a million naira from a single job.

   Two students made five thousand dollars from a hackathon.

   One landed a dev role paying six thousand dollars a month and celebrated with an iPhone 14 Pro Max.

   These results came from strategy, consistency, and the discipline I teach inside Ophir.
5. ![Building Quivira](data:image/jpeg;base64,<inline 106863 bytes stripped>)

   Now

   Now

   ### Building Quivira

   Fast forward to today.

   I am 25 and my life looks nothing like the boy crying silently in the dark.

   I have closed a thirteen thousand dollar marketing deal.

   I have driven over one hundred million dollars in trading volume as a KOL and affiliate for Bybit.

   I was the marketing manager for Adashe.

   I bought myself a Lexus GX460 2020 model. I built a lifestyle for my parents so they never need to stress again.

   I own properties.

   I have over fifty thousand dollars in liquid funds.

   And I still wake up hungry for more.

   My work today cuts across storytelling, crypto marketing, data analysis, blockchain education, community building, and strategic partnerships.
