So do not go and learn another skill yet
----------------------------------------

This is what most people do. Learn a skill. Post about it. Wait.

Then nothing happens, so they decide the problem was the skill, and go and learn another one. Two years later they have four skills and the same bank balance.

The skill was never the problem.

I spoke to a developer this month who has built a live tax system a company paid him for every month, for five months. Real money, real product. When that one client ran out of cash his income went to zero, because everything he had ever built belonged to that one man. He does not need a fifth skill. He needs a second buyer.

That is the gap. Not talent, not effort, not information. Nobody ever handed you the second half.

What makes this different
-------------------------

You are not left alone with it

Seven parts. Seven weeks. One live session each, where we build that week’s piece together.

I asked people directly: a done-for-you kit, or live weekly sessions where we work through it together. It was not close. One of them put it better than I could: *“weekly calls where we build it is better.”* Another said it would be *“well tailored for those who need it, knowing what would really work or not.”*

One person voted for the kit, and he was right about one thing, so let me say it plainly: the templates still come. They just come inside the calls, where I can see your actual skill and your actual situation instead of handing you a blank worksheet and hoping.

That last part is not a guess either. I sent the first tool to somebody who told me she did not know what her skill was. She never filled it in. A blank worksheet is exactly what that person cannot use.
