How to Create a Cinematic Comic Book Ad for Your Brand Using ChatGPT
====================================================================

From: How to Create a Comic Book Ad with ChatGPT

Example comic book ad

You have seen those cinematic comic book ads on Instagram and TikTok. The ones that tell a story in 6 panels, look like they cost thousands to produce, and get people to actually stop scrolling.

You can make one in 5 minutes. For free. Using ChatGPT.

This guide gives you the exact template. Just fill in your story, your brand name, and your offer. Upload your reference photo to ChatGPT. Paste the prompt. Hit enter. Done.

---

What You Need
-------------

* A ChatGPT Plus account (GPT-4o with image generation)
* One clear reference photo of yourself (or whoever the main character is)
* Your brand story (use the fill-in-the-blank guide below)

---

Step 1: Fill In Your Story
--------------------------

Before you touch ChatGPT, answer these 5 questions. Write short, simple answers. These become your comic panels.

**1. THE PROBLEM (Panels 1-2):**

What bad thing happened to you or your audience? What was the struggle? What pain did you feel?

Your answer: [Write 1-2 sentences. Example: "I fell sick and my income stopped because I had no system."]

**2. THE REALIZATION (Panel 3):**

What moment made you decide to change? What hit you?

Your answer: [Write 1 sentence. Example: "I realized I was one sickness away from zero."]

**3. THE SOLUTION (Panel 4):**

What did you build or discover? What changed everything?

Your answer: [Write 1-2 sentences. Example: "I built an AI system that creates content and makes money without me."]

**4. THE OFFER (Panel 5):**

What is your product or service? What are the prices or tiers? What do people get?

Your answer: [Write the details. Example: "AI Content Mastery course. 5 tiers from N15,500 to N306,000."]

**5. THE CLOSE (Panel 6):**

What is your call to action? What should people do next? What is the one line that sticks?

Your answer: [Write it. Example: "Body no be firewood. Build your system."]

---

Step 2: Pick Your Outfit
------------------------

The comic will show your character transforming. You need two looks:

**Struggle outfit (Panels 1-3):** What you wore at your lowest point.

Example: Plain white t-shirt. Hospital gown. Old hoodie. Rumpled shirt.

**Power outfit (Panels 4-6):** What you wear when you mean business.

Example: Dark button-up shirt, grey pinstripe blazer, gold-frame sunglasses, gold watch, lapel pin.

Write down both outfits in detail. Colors, accessories, everything. ChatGPT needs specifics.

---

Step 3: Upload Your Photo and Paste This Prompt
-----------------------------------------------

Open ChatGPT. Upload your reference photo. Then copy and paste the template below. Replace everything in [BRACKETS] with your answers from Step 1 and Step 2.

---

THE PROMPT TEMPLATE
-------------------

Copy

```
Using the person in my reference photo, create a full-page colored comic book illustration in 9:16 vertical format with 6 dynamic connected panels telling an emotional story. Use cinematic comic book art style with bold ink outlines, halftone dot textures, ink splatter strokes, speed lines, dramatic lighting, and high contrast. Vibrant colors with dark backgrounds, red and gold accents. White dialogue bubbles with clean bold black text. Hand-drawn sound effect text. Dramatic perspective shifts between panels. Every panel must show the SAME person from the reference photo with consistent facial features.

PANEL 1 (top, wide panel):
The character is [DESCRIBE YOUR STRUGGLE SCENE. Where are you? What are you doing? What does it look like?]. He/She wears [STRUGGLE OUTFIT]. [DESCRIBE THE ENVIRONMENT: dark room, hospital, empty office, etc.]. Muted, desaturated colors. Sad lighting.
Dialogue bubble: "[WHAT SOMEONE SAYS ABOUT YOUR PROBLEM. Example: WHERE IS THE CONTENT?]"
Second dialogue bubble: "[ANOTHER LINE ABOUT THE PROBLEM. Example: BRO, YOUR PAGE IS DEAD.]"
Caption box at top: "[ONE LINE SETTING THE SCENE. Example: LAST YEAR, MY BODY SHUT DOWN.]"

PANEL 2 (two small panels side by side):
LEFT PANEL: Close-up of the character's face showing [EMOTION: pain, worry, frustration, fear]. Sweat or tears visible.
Dialogue bubble: "[CONSEQUENCE 1. Example: MY CONTENT STOPPED.]"
Second bubble: "[CONSEQUENCE 2. Example: MY CLIENTS LEFT.]"

RIGHT PANEL: [A VISUAL THAT SHOWS THE DAMAGE. Example: phone screen showing zero balance, empty inbox, declining graph, unpaid bills].
Dialogue bubble: "[CONSEQUENCE 3. Example: MY MONEY STOPPED.]"
Sound effect text: "[ONE WORD. Example: FLATLINE]"

PANEL 3 (medium dramatic panel):
The character is now [DESCRIBE THE TURNING POINT SCENE. Example: sitting up in bed with laptop, standing in an empty room staring at a screen]. Still wearing [STRUGGLE OUTFIT] but face shows fierce determination. [ADD A VISUAL ELEMENT THAT SHOWS THE START OF CHANGE: laptop glowing, phone with code, notebook with plans].
Dialogue bubble: "[YOUR REALIZATION. Example: I WAS ONE SICKNESS AWAY FROM ZERO.]"
Second bubble: "[YOUR DECISION. Example: SO I STARTED BUILDING FROM MY SICK BED.]"
Sound effect text: "[ACTION SOUND. Example: TAP TAP TAP]"

PANEL 4 (wide cinematic panel, the transformation):
Split scene. LEFT SIDE shows the struggle version fading away in desaturated gray tones. RIGHT SIDE shows the character fully transformed, stepping forward in full color. Now wearing [POWER OUTFIT: describe in detail including colors, accessories, watch, sunglasses, etc.]. Behind the character, [DESCRIBE YOUR SYSTEM/PRODUCT VISUALLY. Example: floating holographic screens showing automated tools, dashboard with green arrows, money notifications]. Golden warm lighting. Ink splatter energy effects.
Dialogue bubble: "[WHAT YOU BUILT. Example: I BUILT AN AI SYSTEM.]"
Second bubble: "[WHAT IT DOES. Example: IT CREATES CONTENT. FINDS CLIENTS. MAKES MONEY.]"
Third bubble: "[THE POWER LINE. Example: EVEN WHEN I SLEEP.]"
Sound effect text: "BOOM"

PANEL 5 (medium panel):
The character in [POWER OUTFIT], standing confidently. Behind them, [DESCRIBE YOUR OFFER VISUALLY. Example: five glowing price tags floating in ascending order, a course interface, product mockups]. [IF YOU HAVE TIERS/PRICES, list them on glowing cards. The most popular one glows brightest in gold].
Dialogue bubble: "[YOUR OFFER LINE 1. Example: I PUT EVERYTHING INTO ONE COURSE.]"
Second bubble: "[YOUR PRODUCT NAME. Example: AI CONTENT MASTERY.]"
Third bubble: "[DETAILS. Example: ZERO TO PRO. 2 WEEKS. 5 LEVELS.]"

PANEL 6 (bottom, wide panel, the close):
The character pointing directly at the viewer with one hand. Intense confident expression. [POWER OUTFIT with sunglasses on]. Bold powerful pose. Ink splatters and speed lines radiate behind like an energy explosion. Dark background with red and gold bursts.
Dialogue bubble: "[YOUR CTA. Example: SELL IT TOO. EARN UP TO 40% COMMISSION.]"
Second bubble in bold stylized text: "[YOUR SIGNATURE LINE. Example: BODY NO BE FIREWOOD.]"
Large impact text across the bottom: "[FINAL WORDS. Example: YOUR MOVE.]"

Style notes: NO watermarks. NO manga style. Western comic book aesthetic like Marvel or DC. Bold, gritty, cinematic. Every panel must clearly show the same person from the reference photo with consistent facial features. The color palette transitions from cold desaturated blues and grays in panels 1-2 to warm vibrant reds and golds in panels 4-6, representing the transformation. Aspect ratio 9:16 HD.
```

Step 4: Remove AI Metadata Before Posting
-----------------------------------------

After ChatGPT generates your image, the file contains hidden metadata that tells platforms it was made by AI. Remove it before posting.

**Option A: Use Python (if you have it)**

Copy

```
from PIL import Image
img = Image.open("your-comic.png")
clean = Image.new(img.mode, img.size)
clean.putdata(list(img.getdata()))
clean.save("your-comic-clean.png")
```

**Option B: Screenshot method**

Take a screenshot of the image at full zoom. Crop it. Save the screenshot as a new file. The screenshot has no AI metadata.

**Option C: Online tool**

Search "remove image metadata online" and upload your file. Download the clean version.

Step 5: Write Your Caption
--------------------------

Use this caption template. Replace the [BRACKETS]:

[YOUR STRUGGLE IN ONE LINE. Example: "Imagine someone you know falls sick tomorrow."]

[WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THEY STOP. Example: "Content stops. Clients leave. Money goes to zero."]

[FLIP IT ON THE READER. Example: "Now flip it. If YOU fell sick, does your money stop too?"]

[YOUR SOLUTION IN ONE LINE. Example: "I built an AI system that works without me."]

[YOUR PRODUCT AND OFFER. Example: "AI Content Mastery. 5 tiers from N15,500."]

[YOUR CTA WITH LINK. Example: "Register here: yourlink.com"]

Examples of Stories That Work
-----------------------------

**Fitness coach:** "I got injured. Could not train clients for 3 months. Income dropped to zero. So I built an online program that sells while I recover."

**Real estate agent:** "The market crashed. Nobody was buying. My phone stopped ringing. So I built a content system that brings leads even in a dead market."

**Fashion brand:** "My supplier disappeared overnight. No stock. No sales. Customers leaving. So I built a pre-order system that collects payment before I produce."

**Freelancer:** "My biggest client fired me on a Monday. 70% of my income gone in one email. So I built a system that keeps 10 clients in my pipeline at all times."

**Content creator:** "I burned out. Could not create for 2 months. Followers dropped. Brand deals cancelled. So I built an AI content system that posts daily without me."

The Psychology Behind This Template
-----------------------------------

This is not just a pretty comic. It uses 5 marketing psychology layers:

**1. Story-first hook.** People do not stop scrolling for ads. They stop for stories. The comic opens with drama, not a product pitch.

**2. Pain-point stacking.** Panels 1-3 pile on the consequences of NOT having a system. The reader feels the weight before the solution appears.

**3. Visual transformation.** The color shift from cold gray to warm gold is not random. It makes the viewer FEEL the change. Their brain associates your product with that warm, powerful feeling.

**4. Aspirational identity.** The power outfit in panels 4-6 is not vanity. It is visual proof that the character (and by extension, the viewer) can become someone different.

**5. Natural product placement.** The offer appears in panel 5, AFTER the story earns attention. It does not feel like an ad. It feels like the logical next step.
