What This Prompt Creates
------------------------

A 15-second photorealistic CGI cinematic clip of a single character flying over a dark, violent ocean toward a colossal humanoid water entity. The character battles the giant, charges an energy sphere, dives through its core, and detonates it in a massive radial explosion. The camera pulls back to reveal the full destruction scale.

---

What the Full Prompt Covers
---------------------------

* **Visual Style** direction (lens, color palette, grain, camera behavior)
* **Environment** design (ocean, sky, the water entity structure)
* **Sound** design direction
* **Character** lock with face match and flight physics
* **5-beat shot sequence** (0-3s, 3-6s, 6-9s, 9-12s, 12-15s) with exact camera and action directions

---

Why It Works
------------

The prompt is structured in layers: visual style, environment, sound, character, then shot-by-shot timing. Each layer locks a different dimension so the AI model has no room to drift. The shot sequence uses precise second markers so the pacing feels cinematic, not random.
