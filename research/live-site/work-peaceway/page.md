I built a pharmacy platform that never makes a claim about a drug.

Peaceway Online is a Lagos pharmacy. I built and delivered the platform at no fee, so that there would be a working healthcare build I could show to other pharmacies, clinics and laboratories. It is live at [peacewayonline.com](https://peacewayonline.com).

The problem
-----------

Buying medicine in Lagos means guessing. Guessing whether the advice is real, whether the product is genuine, and whether the trip across town is worth it.

A pharmacy can fix all three. Most have no way to show it online.

What I built
------------

**Three doors, not one.** Customers, staff and suppliers each get their own entry point. A wholesaler and a patient should never see the same screen.

![Three access tiers on peacewayonline.com: a customer portal for checkout, order tracking and reminders, a staff portal for Peaceway admins and internal staff, and a partner portal for wholesalers and suppliers.](/_next/image?url=%2Fproof%2Fpeaceway%2F02-three-portals.webp&w=3840&q=75)

Three role-segregated portals: customer, staff, partner.

**A request flow that admits reality.** You tell it the medicine, strength and how soon you need it. Today, within 24 hours, this week, or just checking. It replies that someone will confirm stock and call you back, because that is what actually happens.

![The Find a Medicine request form showing four urgency options as cards: Today for urgent need, Within 24 Hours by tomorrow, This Week no rush, and Just Checking for availability only.](/_next/image?url=%2Fproof%2Fpeaceway%2F06-request-urgency-options.webp&w=3840&q=75)

Urgency tiers and a stated stock-and-callback model, not a blind checkout.

The part most people miss
-------------------------

Nigerian pharmacy advertising is regulated, and those rules shape what a health brand is allowed to publish.

So the system never makes a claim about a drug. Search the inventory and restricted items show **ask pharmacist** where the price would be. The customer gets routed to a qualified human instead of a blind checkout.

![Telegram inventory search results listing products with dosage strengths. Every visible row shows the words ask pharmacist in place of a price.](/_next/image?url=%2Fproof%2Fpeaceway%2F09b-telegram-inventory-search.webp&w=3840&q=75)

Restricted items route to a pharmacist instead of showing a price.

Every promise on the site is about the business. Genuine supply, pharmacist access, delivery across Lagos. Not one of them is a claim about what a medicine does, because that claim belongs to the regulator and to the pharmacist, never to the marketing.

Designing it that way from the start is cheaper than being told to take it down later.
