Tips That Work
--------------

**1. Lead with the value, not the price.**

Say "This course teaches you to make AI videos, automate content, and charge clients in dollars" instead of "Buy this N76,500 course."

**2. Share your own results.**

If you have taken the course, show what you built. Screenshots, videos, client wins. Real proof converts.

**3. Use stories and DMs.**

Posts get reach. Stories get clicks. DMs close sales. Use all three.

**4. Target the right people.**

Content creators, freelancers, small business owners, students who want to learn AI skills. These are the people who buy.

**5. Be consistent.**

One post will not do it. Share 2 to 3 times per week. Different angles, different platforms.

FAQ
---

**Do I need to buy the course first?**

No. Anyone can become an affiliate. But taking the course helps you sell it better.

**What if someone clicks my link but buys later?**

If they buy within 30 days of clicking your link, you get credited.

**Can I refer myself?**

No. Self-referrals are automatically blocked.

**When do I get paid?**

24 hours after the sale, your commission becomes ready. Once your total ready balance hits N5,000, payout is automatic.

**What if a payout fails?**

It shows as "failed" in your dashboard. The admin retries it. You do not lose the commission.

**Can I change my bank details?**

Contact @Quivira\_Ophir on Telegram to update your bank info.
