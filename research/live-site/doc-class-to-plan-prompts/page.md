Class to Plan: The 3 Prompts
============================

From: UGC Challenge, Day 2

start here

Thank you for commenting.

These are the exact 3 prompts I used in my Day 2 video.

I gave Claude a 5-hour class.

It gave me back simple notes, a brand for every day, and a full 30-day plan.

Use them on any class, course, webinar, PDF or long note you want to turn into something you can actually use.

You need 2 things:

→ Claude (the result shows up as a page you can open, called an artifact)

→ Your class recordings, slides, PDFs or notes

---

Prompt 1: Turn any class or long note into simple pages
-------------------------------------------------------

Use this first.

Paste it, then upload your recordings, slides, PDFs or notes.

Copy

```
I attended a class and I want to turn it into something I can actually use.

Here is what I have:
→ The class recordings or files: [paste the links or upload the videos, PDFs or notes]
→ The slides or notes, if any: [paste or upload]
→ What the class was about: [one line, for example "how to get paid as a UGC creator"]

What I want you to do:

1. Go through everything, frame by frame.
   Take a picture every time the slide or the screen changes.
   Write down everything that was said.

2. For each video or file, make one page. Put each page in an artifact. On each page, give me:
   → Frame by frame: every picture, what was on screen, and what the teacher said at that moment.
   → To-dos: everything I should do, as a checklist.
   → Roadmap: every step-by-step process the teacher gave, in order.
   → Formats: every template, script, structure or example I can copy.
   → Links and names: every website, app, account, tool and platform mentioned.
   → Things to note: the warnings and rules the teacher stressed.
   → Unclear bits: anything you could not hear or read clearly, with the time it happened.

3. Capture the small things people normally skip.
   Every link. Every price. Every name. Every step.
   If the teacher says where to register or apply for something, write the exact steps.

4. Do not guess.
   If you are not sure about a word, a name or a number, put it under Unclear bits.
   Never fill a gap with something that sounds right.

5. Before you start, ask me questions about anything you don't fully understand.
   Ask them one at a time, with options I can pick from.

6. Keep the writing simple.
   Short sentences. Plain words. One idea per line.
   Write it so someone who missed the class can follow it.
```

**No class?** It works on any long thing you keep putting off reading. A PDF, a contract, a long voice note. Change the first line to "I have a long [file] and I want to turn it into something I can actually use."

Prompt 2: Find brands running a campaign right now
--------------------------------------------------

Use this if you're doing a content challenge and need a brand for each day.

Copy

```
I'm doing a 30-day content challenge and I need a brand for each day.

Here is what I have:
→ The challenge prompts: [paste or upload the list of daily prompts]
→ My niche: [for example "AI, crypto, fintech"]
→ Where my audience lives: [for example "Nigeria and worldwide"]
→ Where I post: [for example "TikTok, Instagram, X"]
→ Start date: [date]

What I want you to do:

1. Check every brand live, today. Not from memory.
   For each brand, give me:
   → Is it live and working right now? Link to the official page.
   → Can people in my country use it?
   → The one feature I can film in 30 to 60 seconds, and the exact steps on screen.
   → Any campaign running right now: contest, creator programme, affiliate, ambassador or giveaway.
     The name, how to join, who can join, and the end date.
   → Any risk: shut down, hacked, banned or sued recently.

2. Find the brands with the most clearly active campaigns this week.
   Tell me the end dates so I don't miss them.

3. Only drop a brand if it has closed or stopped serving the public.
   Keep every other brand in a backup list I can swap in.

4. Mix local and international brands if the challenge asks for it.

5. Do not guess.
   If you can't confirm something on the brand's own page, mark it UNVERIFIED.
   Give me the link for every fact.

6. Then match one brand to each day's prompt and show me the full map before writing anything.
   Wait for my yes.
```

Prompt 3: Write the scripts and shot lists
------------------------------------------

Use this after you approve the brand map from Prompt 2.

Copy

```
Now write a script for each day on the brand map I approved.

For every day:
→ One question that keeps the viewer watching until the end.
→ A problem first, then the product as the answer. The product is part of my story, not the story itself.
→ Plain words. My audience includes people who aren't technical.
→ End by teasing tomorrow's video.

Put the script and the camera side by side:
→ Each row: the time, the shot size (wide, medium, close-up, screen), what I do, and the exact words I say in that shot.

Also give me:
→ 2 captions: one for TikTok and Instagram, one for X.
→ Any words on screen as handwritten text over the footage. No graphic cards.

Rules:
→ Never invent a fact about me. Where only I know the answer, write [fill this in].
→ Show the messy parts when I film: setup, bad takes, checking playback.
→ If I rewrite a script myself, keep my words exactly and copy my style for the rest.
```

---

3 things that make these work
-----------------------------

**Upload the real files.** Recordings and slides beat your own summary. The detail you'd skip is usually the detail you need.

**Let it ask you questions first.** Prompt 1 tells it to. Answer them. That's where it stops guessing.

**Keep paid classes private.** The pages are for you. Don't post your teacher's material.
