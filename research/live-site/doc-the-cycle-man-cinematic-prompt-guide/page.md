The Story
---------

Ayaan is a young delivery rider in his early 20s. He rides through a busy modern city during golden hour. He senses danger. A little girl steps near the crosswalk while a truck approaches. Time slows. Ayaan struggles through the slowed world, jumps from his bike, and saves the girl. Time snaps back. The mother runs in, hugs the girl, thanks Ayaan, and says, "You saved her..." Ayaan quietly lifts his bike and says, "The Cycle Man... always shows up." He rides away as dusk arrives. His eyes flicker, and the world glitches for a split second so the ending loops back to the start.

The video is built in Pixar-inspired stylized realism, similar emotional quality to Incredibles 2. High-end 3D animation, not live-action, not photorealistic.

Final Notes
-----------

* Keep the story simple. One danger. One rescue. One emotional aftermath.
* Do not make Ayaan look like a superhero. He should look tired, human, and humble.
* The mother and child moment is what gives the save emotional weight.
* The eye flicker at the end creates the loop and makes people rewatch.
