Step 1: Make your character sheet
---------------------------------

One image of you on a plain white background, in 4 panels: a face close-up, a full-body side view, a front view from the neck down, and a full-body back view.

Wear the exact outfit you want in the video. This sheet is how the AI keeps your face and clothes the same in every second.

```
Create a character reference sheet of the person in my selfie. Seamless white background, soft even studio lighting. Four panels side by side, horizontal layout. Panel 1: an extreme close-up of the face, filling most of the frame. Panel 2: a full-body side profile, head to toe. Panel 3: a front view framed from the neck down, showing only the body and outfit. Panel 4: a full-body back view, head to toe. The face, hair and skin tone are identical to my selfie in every panel. The outfit is exactly the one in my outfit photo, same colours, same fabric. Neutral expression. No other people, no text, no props except what I uploaded.
```

The 8 rules I direct with
-------------------------

**Turn off expansion.** If your tool allows it, disable prompt expansion so you know what is actually being rendered.

**Restate every shot.** Don't say "same man". Repeat the face, clothes, location and lighting.

**Direct the sound.** Don't just say "Nigerian accent". Describe pitch, pace, rhythm and emotion.

**Cut inside the prompt.** Use instructions like "cut to close-up" or "cut to wide" instead of generating every angle separately.

**Describe, don't argue.** Don't say "keep the robot sharp this time". Say exactly what the camera should see.

**Lock the voice.** Give each character one voice description and reuse it exactly.

**Verify the output.** Check frame rate, resolution, duration and aspect ratio before editing.

**Let the frame serve the shot.** POV can be 9:16. CCTV can be 1:1. Wide shots can be 16:9.
