Nike-Inspired Nigerian Football Ad: Updated Prompts and Captions
----------------------------------------------------------------

Updated note: the Kling 3.0 prompt has been shortened to fit the 2500-character limit.

---

Talking Head Script
-------------------

Day 5 of making 30 free ads for my favorite brands.

Today, I'm showing you how I made this Nike-inspired Nigerian football ad using ChatGPT and Kling 3.0.

First, I uploaded two reference images into ChatGPT and pasted my image-generation prompt. ChatGPT used those two images to create a realistic Nigerian football stadium crowd shot while keeping both people's identity accurate.

After that, I took the generated image into Kling 3.0 and pasted my video prompt. That prompt tells Kling how to animate the scene, from the stadium zoom-in to the heart gesture and cheek kiss, while keeping the live Nigerian football broadcast feel.

So the workflow is simple: upload two images into ChatGPT, generate the base image, move that image into Kling, paste the video prompt, set it to 16:9, and generate.

Comment "NIKE" on any platform you watched this on, and I'll send you the PDF with both exact prompts.

---

Workflow
--------

1. Upload 2 reference images into ChatGPT.
2. Paste the ChatGPT image-generation prompt.
3. Generate the base Nigerian football stadium image.
4. Upload that generated image into Kling 3.0.
5. Paste the shortened Kling 3.0 prompt.
6. Set output to 16:9.
7. Use 8 to 10 seconds for the full action.
8. Generate.

---

Kling Timing Guide
------------------

Recommended duration: 8 to 10 seconds.

* 0 to 2 sec: far stadium broadcast zoom starts.
* 2 to 4 sec: camera reaches the couple, they notice the camera.
* 4 to 6 sec: they smile and make the heart symbol.
* 6 to 8 sec: girl turns and kisses him on the cheek.
* 8 to 10 sec: boy reacts with a shy smile while the crowd keeps moving.

---

Exact ChatGPT Prompt
--------------------

Create a photorealistic 16:9 live Nigerian football broadcast screenshot of a young Nigerian couple captured from far away by a stadium zoom camera during a night match.

Use the uploaded couple photos as the exact identity reference for both characters. Preserve the man's face, sunglasses, smile, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, and natural expression. Preserve the woman's face, smile, skin tone, hairstyle, body proportions, and natural expression. Do not beautify, reshape, age-change, slim, enlarge, or change their identities. They should look like the same real people from the reference photos, only seated inside a Nigerian football stadium.

**Scene:**

The couple is sitting among a dense crowd inside a packed Nigerian football stadium during a night match. They are not posing for the camera. They look like they were unexpectedly picked up by a long-range broadcast camera during a live football match.

**Camera style:**

Make it look like a telephoto broadcast zoom shot from across the stadium. Use long-lens compression, slightly flattened perspective, medium-wide crowd framing, mild atmospheric haze, subtle broadcast softness, minor motion blur, slight digital zoom artifacts, realistic compression noise, and live TV sharpness.

**Composition:**

Frame them from the audience section, with other spectators partially blocking the foreground and background. Some heads, shoulders, Nigerian flags, green-and-white jerseys, blue athletic jerseys, scarves, caps, raised hands, and stadium banners should naturally overlap the frame.

**Pose:**

The man has his arm naturally around the woman's shoulder and holds a red-and-silver soda can in his other hand. The couple look happy, warm, candid, and slightly surprised, like they just realized they are on the stadium screen.

**Outfit:**

Restyle them into clean Nike-style Nigerian match-day outfits. The man wears a light blue Nike-style athletic fan shirt or clean football jersey, with a clear Nike swoosh placement, while keeping his sunglasses. The woman wears a blue Nike-style sporty casual top or blue summer match-day outfit, with a clear Nike swoosh placement.

**Branding:**

Add real Nike swoosh logos naturally on the couple's outfits and some nearby fan jerseys. Keep the Nike branding clean, premium, and believable. Do not overuse the logo.

**Lighting:**

Use only ambient stadium lighting and weak spill light from the football pitch. No cinematic spotlight. No dramatic key light. No beauty lighting.

**Background:**

Packed Nigerian football stadium, night match atmosphere, fans in green, white, and blue sporty jerseys, waving Nigerian-style supporter flags, scarves, vuvuzelas, caps, and foam hands. Include LED ribbon boards, stadium seating, blurred crowd movement, and a lively realistic environment.

**Broadcast overlay:**

Add a Nigerian sports TV style broadcast presentation using fictional branding only:

* fictional sports channel watermark, "Naija Sports TV"
* LIVE tag
* modern football scoreboard lower-third
* fictional team names, "Lagos Lions" vs "Abuja Warriors"
* fictional scoreline
* match clock
* half indicator
* possession and shots-on-target stat strip
* fictional player names for goal scorers
* small commentary ticker
* clean layered TV graphics

**Quality:**

Ultra-photorealistic, authentic live sports telecast look, premium sportswear ad feeling, realistic human skin texture, natural fabric details, believable stadium zoom-camera perspective, subtle compression artifacts, slightly imperfect broadcast capture, candid audience reaction moment.

**Negative prompt:**

Do not make it look like a professional portrait photoshoot. Do not make the couple look directly into the camera. Do not use cinematic lighting. Do not isolate them from the crowd. Do not make the faces overly smooth or doll-like. Do not change their smiles. Do not change the man's sunglasses unless necessary. Do not change their facial structure. Do not make them look like different people. Do not distort the Nike logo. Do not overuse the Nike logo. Do not use real Nigerian team names, real player names, real sponsors, or real broadcaster logos. Do not make the scoreboard unreadable or messy. Do not make it look like a front-facing mobile photo. Do not claim this is an official Nike campaign.

Updated Kling 3.0 Prompt (Under 2500 Characters)
------------------------------------------------

Ultra-realistic Nike-inspired Nigerian football live broadcast commercial animation, 16:9.

Use the uploaded image as the exact identity reference for the boy and girl. Keep the same faces, hairstyle, glasses, body shape, expressions, outfits, and seating position. Do not change their identity.

Scene: a packed night-time Nigerian football stadium during a live match. Dense crowd energy, green-white-blue jerseys, Nigerian supporter flags, scarves, caps, vuvuzelas, drums, phone flashlights, LED ribbon boards, concrete stadium seats, and Lagos-style match-night atmosphere.

Camera: start as a far stadium broadcast zoom shot, then slowly zoom in like a real football TV crowd-cam. Use long-lens compression, slight handheld shake, digital zoom softness, motion blur, compression noise, haze, and stadium floodlight glow.

Action: the boy and girl notice the stadium camera. They smile naturally, make a heart symbol together with their hands, then the girl gently turns and gives him a soft cheek kiss. The boy reacts with a shy warm smile.

Style: emotional Nike-inspired sportswear ad energy, youthful, premium, natural, powerful, candid, not staged. Realistic skin, fabric texture, crowd movement, stadium lighting, and live TV broadcast feel.

Add a minimal fictional Nigerian football broadcast overlay: Naija Sports TV, LIVE, Lagos Lions vs Abuja Warriors, 2 - 1, 72:48, LIVE NOW, "WHAT A NIGHT OF FOOTBALL IN LAGOS".

Do not use real Nigerian league branding, real broadcasters, real sponsors, real teams, real players, IPL, cricket, JioCinema, Mumbai Indians, DSLR photoshoot style, plastic skin, over-beautified faces, or isolated couple framing. Keep it like a real Nigerian football crowd-cam moment that accidentally feels like a Nike commercial.

----------------------

3-second ultra-realistic Nike-inspired sports ad outro, 16:9.

Use the uploaded outro image as the exact visual reference. Keep the black stadium background, Nigerian football crowd, floodlights, Nike-style central logo placement, bold JUST DO IT text, green-and-white Nigerian fan energy, and premium sports commercial mood.

**Action:**

Start on the full stadium outro image. Add a quick stadium floodlight flare, subtle crowd movement, floating dust/confetti, and a gentle camera push-in toward the center logo and text. The Nike-style swoosh and JUST DO IT text stay clean, sharp, and centered.

**Audio feel:**

Add crowd roar, short bass hit, and quick fabric-motion whoosh.

**Style:**

Premium Nike-inspired football ad outro, clean, powerful, cinematic, minimal, high-end sportswear commercial finish.
