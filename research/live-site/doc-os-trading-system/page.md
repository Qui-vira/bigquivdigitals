What It Does
------------

My AI scans **50 crypto pairs every 4 hours**.

It checks RSI, MACD, volume, support, resistance, and EMA alignment on each one. Simultaneously.

Only setups with **3+ indicators agreeing** make it to my queue.

---

How My Morning Looks
--------------------

* Wake up. 3 signals already waiting.
* Review each one. Approve or reject.
* Done before coffee.

No chart staring. No 8-hour screen sessions. The machine scans. I decide.

---

Why I Built It
--------------

I was spending 8+ hours a day scanning charts manually. Burning out.

So I built the system to handle the scanning. Now I just make the final call.
