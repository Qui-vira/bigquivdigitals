# The post did 128,000 views. Then what?

That is the question most brands never answer. The post lands, the comments fill up, and a week later there is no list and no conversation. The video was never the asset. What catches the video is the asset.

## The post

A talking-head video with one instruction: comment "Web3 Jobs" and I will send you the guide.

128,000 views. 1,700 comments. 1,400 likes, 267 reposts, 598 bookmarks. Every comment turned into a conversation, and the next day I posted the guide publicly so the loop closed where everyone could see it.

Most brands would have run that as an ad pointing at a form. The comment does more work. It costs the viewer less, it feeds the algorithm, and it gives you a reason to open a DM they asked for.
